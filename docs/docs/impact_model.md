METALFLUX is designed to deliver measurable environmental and public health impact by addressing the root causes of poor waste management and its direct effects on child health in vulnerable communities.

The project aims to reduce unmanaged waste in target communities by up to 40% through structured community reporting and rapid response waste collection systems.

By improving sanitation and reducing environmental pollution, METALFLUX will contribute to a reduction in exposure to waterborne and vector-borne diseases such as cholera, malaria, and typhoid, which disproportionately affect children.

The system will also strengthen community awareness and participation in environmental protection, creating long-term behavioral change.

In addition, the recycling component of METALFLUX will generate economic opportunities by converting waste into reusable materials, supporting local livelihoods while reducing environmental harm.

Overall, METALFLUX contributes to climate resilience, improved public health outcomes, and sustainable urban development.
