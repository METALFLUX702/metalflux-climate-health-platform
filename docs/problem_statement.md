Waste mismanagement is a major environmental and public health challenge in many African communities, particularly in Nigeria. Over 60% of waste is not properly collected or managed, leading to blocked drainage systems, flooding, and environmental pollution.

These conditions create breeding grounds for disease vectors and contribute to outbreaks of cholera, malaria, and typhoid. Children are the most vulnerable group, as they are frequently exposed to contaminated environments.

A major gap exists in real-time monitoring systems that can link environmental waste accumulation to public health risks. Without data-driven insight, early intervention becomes difficult, and communities remain exposed to preventable health threats.
