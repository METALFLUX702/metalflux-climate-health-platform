METALFLUX is a climate-health innovation platform that integrates community-based waste reporting with environmental monitoring and data analytics to reduce public health risks associated with poor sanitation.

The system enables community members to report waste accumulation using mobile tools. This data is collected and analyzed to identify environmental hotspots that are likely to contribute to disease outbreaks such as cholera, malaria, and typhoid.

METALFLUX connects waste management operations with a digital monitoring system that supports real-time decision-making for waste collection and environmental intervention.

The solution also incorporates recycling and circular economy principles by converting collected waste into reusable materials, creating both environmental and economic value.

Through this integrated approach, METALFLUX aims to improve sanitation, reduce disease exposure, and strengthen community-driven environmental health systems.
